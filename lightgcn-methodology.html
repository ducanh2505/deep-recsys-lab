Methodology · Paper-faithful core

Less is the model.

LightGCN removes feature transforms, nonlinear activations and self-connections from graph collaborative filtering. What remains is a normalized bipartite graph, trainable ego embeddings and an even average across propagation depths.

From NGCF to LightGCN

Three removals, one sharper inductive bias.

The paper’s ablations argue that the graph’s neighborhood structure—not generic neural-network complexity—is the useful signal for collaborative filtering.

×

No feature transform

No layer-specific weight matrix. Every propagation step aggregates the same embedding space.

×

No nonlinearity

No ReLU or activation between graph hops; propagation remains a sparse linear operation.

×

No self-connection

The graph diagonal is zero. Layer 0 already preserves the node’s own signal before layer averaging.

Architecture

Propagate. Average. Score.

01 · Graph propagation

Users and movies form the two partitions of an undirected interaction graph. Degrees symmetrically normalize every edge.

E⁽ᵏ⁺¹⁾ = D−1/2 A D−1/2 E⁽ᵏ⁾

The local implementation stores A as a coalesced sparse COO tensor in FP32 on CPU.

02 · Layer combination & score

Layer 0 through K receive equal weight. A user–movie affinity is their final embedding inner product.

e = 1/(K+1) Σk=0…K e⁽ᵏ⁾   ·   ŷui = euᵀei

All-ranking evaluation scores the full catalog and masks every item already observed for that user.

03 · BPR objective
L = −Σ log σ(ŷui − ŷuj) + λ ‖E⁽⁰⁾‖²

Each triple contains a user, one train-positive movie and one uniformly drawn movie absent from that training graph. L2 regularization applies to ego embeddings only—not propagated embeddings. Validation and test edges never enter negative sampling during model selection.

Local protocol

A test fold sealed until the end.

Ratings at least 4.0 become implicit positives. Users need five positives; duplicate user–movie pairs collapse before deterministic per-user splitting with seed 98765.

Prepare

Filter positives, split each user, then define the catalog from train only.

Filter cold start

Remove 417 validation and 399 test edges whose movies never enter train.

Successive halving

Nine layer/L2 pairs run 100 steps; the top three continue to 300.

Early stop

The winner runs to 4,000 max, checked every 50 after step 500, patience ten.

Retrain & test once

Restart on train + validation for the chosen step; only then unseal test.

Paper comparison

Same core method. Different experimental surface.

Do not compare the local MovieLens score numerically with the rows below. Gowalla, Yelp2018 and Amazon-Book have different users, catalogs, sparsity and splits; a delta would be misleading.

Shared with the paper
  • 64-dimensional Xavier-initialized embeddings
  • Loop-free symmetric normalized graph
  • Linear propagation and uniform layer averaging
  • Inner-product scoring, BPR and ego-only L2
  • All-ranking Recall@20 and NDCG@20
Local resource adaptations
  • MovieLens-20M transfer dataset, not the paper’s three datasets
  • CPU FP32 with eight threads; no MPS sparse path
  • Batch 262,144 instead of 1,024 (Amazon-Book: 2,048)
  • At most 4,000 winner steps inside eight hours, replacing the paper’s up-to-1,000-epoch schedule
  • Clean final retrain and one recorded test access
